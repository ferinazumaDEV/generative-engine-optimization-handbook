## What's new in GEO

### Publisher controls & standards

- **[Google] The opt-out control for AI Overviews and AI Mode went worldwide on 31 Aug 2026.** Google's own documentation now states: *"As of August 31, 2026, we've rolled out this control to all websites worldwide."* The **Search generative AI control** in Search Console governs whether your content *"can appear in Search generative AI features, including showing up as links and helping to ground AI responses"* — covering **AI Overviews, AI Mode, and generative AI features in Discover**. Google is explicit that it *"isn't used as a ranking or inclusion signal affecting other parts of Search"*, and equally explicit about the cost of using it: *"You won't receive any traffic or impressions from these features."* ([Google Search Console Help](https://support.google.com/webmasters/answer/16908024) · [Search Engine Land, 31 Aug 2026](https://searchengineland.com/google-search-console-ai-performance-reports-and-search-generative-ai-control-rolling-out-globally-486269))
  - Why it matters: **this is the single most consequential item of the week for practitioners**, and it resolves a caveat the handbook has been carrying since June. There is now, globally, a way to leave AI Overviews and AI Mode **without** touching normal rankings or snippets — the thing that did not exist when `nosnippet` was the only lever. For almost every reader the correct action is *no action* (you want to be in AI answers), but the control finally makes the choice a real one instead of a blunt trade-off. Chapters [02 · The Engines](../docs/02-engines.md#google--ai-overviews-ai-mode--gemini) and [04 · Technical GEO](../docs/04-technical.md#snippet-directives--and-the-new-opt-out-toggle) updated.
- **[Cloudflare] The 15 Sep 2026 crawler default is 8 days out and unchanged.** Re-verified at source this week: *"On September 15, 2026, we'll be setting new defaults for each of these three classifications"* and *"For all new domains onboarding to Cloudflare, the categories of Training and Agent will be blocked by default on the pages that display ads, while Search will remain allowed by default."* ([Cloudflare, 1 Jul 2026](https://blog.cloudflare.com/content-independence-day-ai-options/))
  - Why it matters: no news is the news — the date did not slip. If you are on Cloudflare and onboarding a domain after next Monday, confirm the **Search** crawlers you want are allowed before the default reaches you.
- **[IETF] AIPREF: no movement this week.** Both drafts remain at the revisions the handbook already cites — [`draft-ietf-aipref-vocab-07`](https://datatracker.ietf.org/doc/draft-ietf-aipref-vocab/) and [`draft-ietf-aipref-attach-05`](https://datatracker.ietf.org/doc/draft-ietf-aipref-attach/), both dated **19 Aug 2026**, both still in **WG Document** state and both now showing an expiry of **20 Feb 2027**. No new revision, no working-group last call.
  - Why it matters: the `train-ai` / `search` split and the `Content-Usage` header are still pre-consensus drafts. Nothing to change; recorded so the timeline shows the check was made rather than skipped.

### AI answer engines

- **[Perplexity] The Sonar API retirement holds at 27 Sep 2026 — and the notice is not in the changelog.** Perplexity's migration guide states *"Sonar will be supported until September 27, 2026"*, and the announcement thread of 13 Aug 2026 puts it as *"Sonar endpoints are fully available today and retire on September 27, in 45 days."* Enumerating the announcements category shows nothing after 13 Aug touches the deadline (newest posts: Stripe Projects, 3 Sep; Connectors in Agent API, 27 Aug). ([docs.perplexity.ai — migrate from Sonar](https://docs.perplexity.ai/docs/agent-api/migrate-from-sonar/overview) · [community.perplexity.ai, 13 Aug 2026](https://community.perplexity.ai/t/sonar-is-moving-to-the-agent-api/5802))
  - Why it matters: **under three weeks left** for anyone querying `sonar`/`sonar-pro` chat completions for citation or visibility tracking. The gotcha worth passing on: the retirement notice appears in the community announcement and the migration guide but **not in the developer changelog**, so a team monitoring only the changelog will be caught out.
- **[Perplexity] The "Search SDK" reported on 24 Aug is real and vendor-documented** — last week we flagged it as an unverified third-party tracker rumour; that is now resolved. The `pplx-srch-sdk` Python package is named in Perplexity's own GitHub org repo, whose Agent Skill *"Installs and uses the `pplx-srch-sdk` Python SDK for live web search, concurrent query fan-out, and query-relevant page snippets"*. ([github.com/perplexityai/api-platform-developers](https://github.com/perplexityai/api-platform-developers) · [docs.perplexity.ai](https://docs.perplexity.ai/guides/perplexity-sdk-search))
  - Why it matters: "Search as Code" is now an installable retrieval surface, so agents can run their own fan-out-and-rank pipeline over Perplexity's index instead of accepting one fixed ranked list. Useful for building citation-measurement harnesses.
- **[OpenAI] GPT-6 Astra ships (3 Sep 2026), with browsing as the headline claim.** OpenAI announced Astra as setting *"a new state of the art for computer use, browsing, software engineering, cybersecurity, science, and professional work."* On the browsing benchmark **BrowseComp**, published results put Astra at **91.5%** against GPT-5.6 Sol's **90.4%** — a gap of 1.1 points. Rollout is to Plus, Pro, Business and Enterprise plus the API, Azure and Bedrock. **OpenAI has not said whether or when Astra serves ChatGPT search answers.** ([OpenAI Developer Community announcement, 3 Sep 2026](https://community.openai.com/t/introducing-gpt-6-astra-the-most-intelligent-and-aligned-model-in-the-world/1394703)) `> ⚠️ openai.com returns HTTP 403 to automated fetchers, so the primary post at openai.com/index/gpt-6-astra/ could not be retrieved directly; the OpenAI-operated community announcement is cited in its place.`
  - Why it matters: a frontier model whose headline is browsing is the most likely near-term cause of a shift in how many sub-queries ChatGPT fires and which pages survive into the citation set — but a **~1-point** browsing gain is not evidence of a step change in retrieval. Nobody has measured Astra's citation behaviour yet. Don't rewrite a strategy on it.
- **[OpenAI] ChatGPT Ads reached a $1B annualized run rate and opened self-serve buying across India, Europe and MEA (31 Aug 2026)**, taking availability past 40 countries. OpenAI's stated position is unchanged: ads *"are always clearly labeled and separate from the chatbot's answers. Ads do not influence those answers."* ([PYMNTS, 31 Aug 2026](https://www.pymnts.com/news/artificial-intelligence/2026/openai-says-ad-business-reaches-1-billion-run-rate/), secondary coverage quoting OpenAI's post)
  - Why it matters: the separation of the paid surface from the organic citation set is now a **load-bearing vendor claim** attached to a fast-growing revenue line. The field should be independently testing it, not assuming it. Organic GEO remains a separate discipline.
- **[OpenAI] ChatGPT gained read-only connectors to official healthcare databases (1 Sep 2026)** — Epic EHR integration for US clinicians plus a "Healthcare Public Data" plugin wiring in official public sources; those named in coverage include ClinicalTrials.gov, CMS Coverage, RxNorm, DailyMed, PubMed and openFDA. ([TechCrunch, 1 Sep 2026](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/01/chatgpt-health-adds-epic-integration-for-clinicians-to-import-patient-data/), secondary)
  - Why it matters: a concrete instance of the pattern that matters most for GEO — for an entire vertical, ChatGPT is pointed at **licensed structured connectors instead of ranked web pages**, which caps how much open-web content in that vertical can be cited at all. Watch for the same move in finance and law.
- **[Perplexity] Hybrid Compute on Mac (1 Sep 2026) moves part of the agent off the network.** Cloud models handle research and planning while a local model on Apple silicon handles steps touching private files, gated by a 0.6B on-device PII detector. The underlying inference engine, **Lily**, was open-sourced on 2 Sep. ([github.com/perplexityai/pplx-garden](https://github.com/perplexityai/pplx-garden) — the engine repo is the verifiable artefact; Perplexity's own blog post returned HTTP 403 to automated fetching, and the Hybrid Compute details were corroborated through 9to5Mac and MarkTechPost coverage of 1 Sep 2026)
  - Why it matters: a measurement consequence, not a content one. **Agent steps executed on-device never appear as `PerplexityBot` or `Perplexity-User` fetches in your server logs**, so log-based crawl analysis will increasingly undercount what Perplexity actually does with your content. See [06 · Measurement](../docs/06-measurement.md#layer-3--the-crawl-layer-server-logs).

### Research & data

- **[Study] Ahrefs (2 Sep 2026): the AI Overviews citation pool is dominated by platforms, not publishers.** Across **over 3 million US queries**, the most-cited domains are **YouTube 22.9%**, **Reddit 18.5%**, **Facebook 10.1%**, **Google 8.8%** and **Instagram 5.6%**. Methodology, in Ahrefs' words: *"The numbers come from Ahrefs' Brand Radar, which tracks the responses AI platforms give to a large, search-backed set of real user queries."* (vendor study). ([Ahrefs](https://ahrefs.com/blog/most-cited-domains-ai-overviews/))
  - Why it matters: two things. First, it sets the realistic ceiling — roughly two-thirds of AI Overview citations go to five platforms, so an independent site is competing for the remaining tail, which is exactly where last week's *"34.5% of citations come from sites with DA under 40"* finding lives. Second, **Reddit at 18.5% in Google AI Overviews sits in direct tension with the reported collapse of Reddit's share in ChatGPT** — good evidence that citation dynamics are engine-specific and that a shift on one engine should never be generalised to "AI search".

### Negative findings (things that turned out not to be true, or not to have changed)

Recording these deliberately: a refuted claim is as useful as a confirmed one, and cheaper to act on.

- **Reddit did *not* change its `robots.txt` in August 2026.** Several GEO write-ups attributed the drop in Reddit's ChatGPT citation share to a fresh blanket block. The primary artefact refutes it: `reddit.com/robots.txt` today is a seven-line file whose only rule is `User-agent: *` / `Disallow: /`, and the Wayback captures of **16 Aug 2026**, **14 Apr 2026** and **1 Jul 2024** are byte-identical to it. There are no `Allow` rules and no named user-agents. ([Wayback capture, 16 Aug 2026](http://web.archive.org/web/20260816004512/https://www.reddit.com/robots.txt))
  - Why it matters: it removes one of the two circulating causal stories, and it is a reminder that **a `robots.txt` causal claim costs one archive lookup to falsify** — do that before building strategy on it.
- **No new evidence on the ChatGPT `site:` fan-out spike or the Reddit citation collapse.** Chasing last week's open item: nothing was published between 31 Aug and 6 Sep. The originating vendor's newest reports are both dated 18 Aug 2026, and the `site:`-operator report itself (10 Aug) has had no follow-up — so the figures (fan-out `site:` usage ~0.37% → 16.8%; Reddit share 3.83% → 0.52%) remain **a single-vendor, un-replicated measurement with no stated sample size**, and the six-day gap to the sharper 14 Aug drop is still unexplained. ([Promptwatch data index](https://promptwatch.com/data))
  - Why it matters: the honest status is **unchanged and unconfirmed**. It stays in *Watching*, not in a chapter.
- **Perplexity's crawler documentation is unchanged.** Still exactly two agents: `PerplexityBot` (respects `robots.txt`, *"not used to crawl content for AI foundation models"*) and `Perplexity-User`, which *"generally ignores robots.txt rules"* because a user requested the fetch. No new user-agent, no policy change, no new crawler dispute in the window. ([Perplexity Docs — Crawlers](https://docs.perplexity.ai/docs/resources/perplexity-crawlers), retrieved 2026-09-07)
  - Why it matters: `robots.txt` alone is still not a control surface for `Perplexity-User` — that needs edge or WAF rules. The handbook's existing guidance stands.

## Watching / needs verification

- **[Preprint] Query language, not exit IP, may decide which market's brands ChatGPT names.** [arXiv:2608.30052](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.30052) (submitted **30 Aug 2026**, one day before the window) reports 234 runs across 11 cells, four exit countries and six query languages: *"across the four cells where the query language is the official language of the exit country, a global supplier appeared in 1 of 24 runs"*, and in the two English cells on the Istanbul and Tallinn connections *"a local supplier appeared in 0 of 12 runs."* The top recommendation was unstable on four of six prompts in each arm. **Single author with a GEO-vendor dual affiliation (Rankfor.AI OÜ); preprint, not peer-reviewed; n is small.** Genuinely interesting for multilingual strategy and for setting a floor on GEO sample sizes — but it needs replication before it informs guidance, and the vendor affiliation must be disclosed wherever it is cited.
