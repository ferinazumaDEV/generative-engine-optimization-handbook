## What's new in GEO

### AI answer engines

- **[Google] Publishers can now embed a one-click "Preferred Source" button.** Readers who click it mark your site as a *Preferred Source*; your content then gets a "preferred" badge and surfaces more in **Top Stories, AI Overviews, and AI Mode** for that reader. Implementation code is in Search Central; Google reports **600,000+** sources already selected (Telegraph, Guardian, Daily Mail among early adopters). ([Press Gazette, 24 Aug 2026](https://pressgazette.co.uk/platforms/google-preferred-source-article-users-curate-sources-top-stories-ai-overview-search/))
  - Why it matters: a genuinely *new*, publisher-controlled visibility lever inside AI Overviews / AI Mode — the first "do this on your site to influence AI-answer placement" control Google has shipped. Drive reader opt-ins where relevant.
- **[Google] "Developing-topic" link carousels rolled into AI Mode answers.** Horizontal carousels of clickable article links (already in AI Overviews) now appear between sections of AI Mode responses for trending topics, surfacing preferred/original-coverage sources. Confirmed by Google's Robby Stein. ([Search Engine Land, 26 Aug 2026](https://searchengineland.com/google-adds-link-carousels-for-developing-topics-in-ai-mode-485884))
  - Why it matters: a new, prominent inline link slot inside AI Mode — original-coverage and preferred sources can win clicks the answer text would otherwise absorb.
- **[ChatGPT] OpenAI turned on ads across 31 European markets (40 total) from 24 Aug** — on Free and Go plans only, non-personalized at launch (GDPR), and **clearly labelled and separate from answers**; OpenAI states advertising "does not influence the answers ChatGPT provides." ([Euronews, 21 Aug 2026](https://www.euronews.com/next/2026/08/21/chatgpt-to-start-showing-ads-across-europe-next-week))
  - Why it matters: a *paid* in-conversation lever now spans the EU, but **organic citation remains a separate discipline** — GEO for organic answers is unaffected by the ad surface.
- **[Perplexity] Sonar API endpoints will be retired on 27 Sep 2026** in favor of the new unified **Agent API**; Perplexity's docs state "Sonar will be supported until September 27, 2026." ([docs.perplexity.ai](https://docs.perplexity.ai/docs/sonar/quickstart))
  - Why it matters: anyone querying Perplexity programmatically for citation/visibility tracking must re-point integrations before the deadline (citation-token formats differ across the new presets).
- **[Bing] Microsoft is testing renaming the "Copilot Search" answer box to "AI Overviews"** on Bing SERPs (Copilot logo unchanged) — an early UI/terminology test, first spotted on mobile. ([Search Engine Roundtable, 27 Aug 2026](https://www.seroundtable.com/bing-testing-copilot-search-ai-overviews-41953.html))
  - Why it matters: minor for now, but it signals Bing aligning its AI-answer surface with Google's terminology; watch how source links are presented on the box.

### Publisher controls & standards

- **[IETF] The AIPREF working group advanced two drafts (19 Aug) toward a standard "crawl but don't train" signal.** [`draft-ietf-aipref-vocab-07`](https://datatracker.ietf.org/doc/draft-ietf-aipref-vocab/) defines the preference vocabulary — **`train-ai`** (modifying model parameters) vs **`search`** (linking/excerpting to direct users to the source) — and [`draft-ietf-aipref-attach-05`](https://datatracker.ietf.org/doc/draft-ietf-aipref-attach/) defines how to attach those preferences: a **`Content-Usage` HTTP response header** and a parallel **`Content-Usage` rule in `robots.txt`**.
  - Why it matters: the emerging *standard* successor to today's ad-hoc `GPTBot`/`Google-Extended` tokens — one machine-readable way to permit answer-engine "search" use while denying training, per path. **Still pre-consensus** (the vocab draft says so explicitly); watch the datatracker pages. Added to [04 · Technical GEO](../docs/04-technical.md#robotstxt-and-ai-crawler-control).
- **[Cloudflare] From 15 Sep 2026, new domains get Training + Agent crawlers blocked by default on ad-bearing pages** (Search bots stay allowed), reasoning that an ad signals a page meant for a human. Multi-purpose crawlers like `Googlebot` are judged across *all* their behaviors, so blocking "training" can catch a crawler you also rely on for search. Existing domains keep their current settings. ([Cloudflare, 1 Jul 2026](https://blog.cloudflare.com/content-independence-day-ai-options/))
  - Why it matters: if you're on Cloudflare, **confirm the search crawlers you want are still allowed** — a platform default is a silent reason to vanish from AI answers.
- **[llms.txt] v2 of the spec (10 Aug) adds discovery via HTML `<link rel>` and HTTP `Link:` headers** — but **still no major answer engine confirms reading `llms.txt`** for retrieval or citation; an Ahrefs sample found ~97% of published files never fetched. ([llmstxt.org](https://llmstxt.org/) · [Ahrefs](https://ahrefs.com/blog/llmstxt-study/))
  - Why it matters: unchanged verdict — low cost, useful for AI *dev tools* consuming your docs, not a proven citation lever. The handbook's [04 · Technical](../docs/04-technical.md#llmstxt-the-emerging-and-unproven-convention) stance holds.

### Research & data (fresh citable numbers)

- **[Study] Featured "AI Citation Report" (25 Aug): 34.5% of AI citations came from sites with Domain Authority under 40.** Across **22,881** citations from **405** GEO audits (2 Jun – 21 Aug; ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, Gemini) (vendor study). ([GlobeNewswire](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/08/25/3350347/0/en/featured-launches-geo-audit-to-show-brands-which-publications-ai-search-engines-cite-and-which-ones-to-pitch.html))
  - Why it matters: more evidence engines cite by *fit*, not classic domain authority — supports niche/topical strategies.
- **[Study] GPO "State of Search & AI" (10 Aug): being cited ≠ being recommended.** Across **50,000+** commercial keywords, a brand cited *inside* an AI answer was still left out of the model's recommendation **69%** of the time; separately, **68% of US Google searches ended without a click in early 2026** (up from 60% in 2024) (agency-reported). ([gpo.com](https://gpo.com/blog/august-2026-state-of-search-ai/))
  - Why it matters: reframes the success metric — appearing as a citation is not the same as being the *recommended* brand.
- **[Study] Brainlabs / Digiday (25 Aug): the traffic trade-off, quantified.** Across **54 advertisers** (Jan 2025 – Apr 2026), organic search sessions fell **10.5%** (140.1M → 125.4M) while AI-platform referral sessions rose **163%** and AI-referred key events rose **335%**, with AI referrals converting **~1.5×** the organic rate (agency-reported, single dataset; see [06 · Measurement](../docs/06-measurement.md)). ([Digiday](https://digiday.com/marketing/in-graphic-detail-how-ai-search-has-impacted-the-web-traffic-of-over-50-advertisers/))
  - Why it matters: less top-of-funnel organic volume, but higher-intent AI-referred visitors — reinforces optimizing for AI answers over raw click counts.
- **[Paper] SIGIR 2026 "What Gets Cited: Competitive GEO in AI Answer Engines" (arXiv:2605.25517, 25 May):** a **252,000-trial** factorial across **six LLMs** and **18 content factors** finds **topical relevance and list position** drive the first citation, **price/recency help consistently**, and **formatting-only edits barely move it**. ([arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.25517))
  - Why it matters: the most rigorous causal-style evidence to date on which on-page levers actually earn citations — and a caution against cosmetic "GEO tweaks." Added to [07 · Research & Case Studies](../docs/07-research-cases.md#part-2--research-building-on-geo-the-wider-literature).
